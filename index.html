精准节拍器 · 帮助与支持

「精准节拍器」是为练琴、唱歌准备的节拍器，自带校音器。每一下都按声音采样点精确排好，拍子不会越走越快或越走越慢。

常见问题

怎么设置速度？

拖动滑块，或点「−」「+」逐个调整（按住不放可连续调）；也可以跟着音乐连续点几下「TAP」，App 会按点击间隔算出速度。

每小节 0 拍和 1 拍有什么区别？

两者都是每拍响一下：1 拍时每一下都是重拍的音，0 拍时每一下都是普通的音，没有重拍。

锁屏后还会响吗？

会。锁屏或切到别的 App（比如看谱）时节拍器继续响，可以在锁屏界面或控制中心停止。来电、闹钟等打断后会自动停下，不会突然自己响起来。

校音器没有反应？

  • 确认已允许麦克风权限：iOS「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 麦克风」中打开「精准节拍器」。
  • 尽量在安静的环境使用，手机离乐器近一些，每个音奏得稳一点、长一点。
  • 如果正在发出参考音，校音器会暂停读数，关掉参考音即可。

标准音 A = 440 是什么意思？

指 A4（中央 C 上方的 la）的频率是 440 Hz，其他音都按它推算。乐团常用 442，可以在校音器里的「A = 440」处调整（438–445 Hz）。

Precision Metronome · Support

Precision Metronome is a metronome with a built-in tuner for practicing an instrument or singing. Every click is placed on an exact sample grid, so the beat never speeds up or slows down.

FAQ

How do I set the tempo?

Drag the slider, or tap − and + to step one BPM at a time (hold to repeat). You can also tap TAP along with the music a few times and the app works out the tempo.

What's the difference between 0 and 1 beats per bar?

Both click once per beat. With 1, every click uses the accented sound; with 0, every click uses the normal sound and nothing is accented.

Does it keep playing when the screen locks?

Yes. The metronome keeps playing when the screen locks or you switch to another app (to read sheet music, for example). Stop it from the Lock Screen or Control Center. After an interruption such as a call or alarm it stays stopped instead of starting again on its own.

The tuner doesn't respond

  • Make sure microphone access is on: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone → Precision Metronome.
  • Use it somewhere quiet, keep the phone close to your instrument, and hold each note steadily.
  • While the reference tone is playing, the tuner pauses its readings; turn the tone off to see them.

What does A = 440 mean?

It sets A4 (the A above middle C) to 440 Hz; every other note is calculated from it. Many orchestras tune to 442 — tap "A = 440" in the tuner to change it (438–445 Hz).
