隐私政策

精准节拍器（Precision Metronome）· 生效日期：2026 年 11 月 1 日

「精准节拍器」由 Zhao Yongbiao 开发。我们重视你的隐私：本 App 不收集、不存储、不分享任何个人数据。

麦克风

只有校音器使用麦克风，用来识别你演奏或演唱的音高。声音只在你的设备上实时分析，不会录音、不会保存，也不会上传到任何服务器。App 切到后台时校音器会自动关闭；节拍器本身不使用麦克风。你可以随时在 iOS「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 麦克风」中关闭权限。

设置

速度、每小节拍数、节奏型、标准音等设置只保存在你的设备上，用于下次打开时恢复，不会发送到任何地方。

不做的事情

  • 没有账号注册与登录
  • 没有 App 内购买
  • 没有广告，没有第三方统计或分析工具
  • 不追踪你在其他 App 或网站中的活动

儿童

本 App 不收集任何人的个人数据，包括儿童。

政策变更

如果本政策有变化，我们会更新本页面并修改上方的生效日期。

Privacy Policy

Precision Metronome · Effective November 1, 2026

Precision Metronome is developed by Zhao Yongbiao. The app does not collect, store or share any personal data.

Microphone

Only the tuner uses the microphone, to detect the pitch you play or sing. Audio is analyzed on your device in real time and is never recorded, stored or uploaded. The tuner turns off when the app goes to the background; the metronome itself does not use the microphone. You can turn off microphone access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone.

Settings

Your tempo, beats per bar, rhythm pattern and reference pitch are stored only on your device so they are restored next time. They are never sent anywhere.

What we don't do

  • No account sign-up or sign-in
  • No in-app purchases
  • No ads, and no third-party analytics
  • No tracking across other apps or websites

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
